ABOUT الحجر يتحدث بتاريخ الأردن

A homeland told through stone

When Jordanian stone speaks, it tells the story of a homeland born from glory and purpose—a homeland guided by the Hashemites with integrity and devotion.

From Madaba, the bride of Jordanian tourism, and from the depth of our pure soil, the project “الحجر يتحدث بتاريخ الأردن” begins as a digital and national initiative. It affirms that our national identity reaches beyond tourism and heritage, rooted in the enduring values of the Amman Message: moderation, fraternity and shared humanity.

As we take this step, we draw inspiration from the historic Hashemite custodianship of the Islamic and Christian holy sites in Al-Quds Al-Sharif, carried by His Majesty King Abdullah II Ibn Al Hussein with wisdom and courage. His continued support for national and creative initiatives gives our young women and men the confidence to create and innovate, knowing that serving Jordan and its heritage is a natural extension of the dignity and honor carried by the Hashemite family.
